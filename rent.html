FAQ

Common questions

How far in advance do I need to book?

Summer weekends often book two to three weeks out. Weekdays and shoulder season are usually available within a few days. We confirm every request within 2 hours.

What do I need to provide?

A reasonably level spot we can back a trailer into, and that's about it. We bring the firewood, the ladle and bucket, and the know-how. Towels are on you.

How hot does it get?

Hot enough for a proper sauna. The wood stove takes 45 to 60 minutes to come up to temperature, and from there you control the heat with the stove and the ladle.

Can we use it during a fire ban?

The sauna stove is an enclosed wood-burning appliance, but campfire bans and local rules vary. We check the current regional restrictions before every delivery and will tell you straight if your date is affected. If a ban gets in the way, we'll work with you to move your booking.

What's the cold plunge add-on?

A galvanized stock trough, delivered alongside the trailer and filled on site, for $75. If you're on the lake, you already have the best cold plunge there is.

What if it rains?

Saunas are better in the rain. The trailer is fully enclosed, and we deliver in any weather the roads allow.
