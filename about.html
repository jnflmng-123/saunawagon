The Idea

An old horse trailer.
A simple idea.

We've been around the Shuswap our whole lives. We know the lake, the cabins, the long summer evenings, and the people who come back every year because there's nowhere else like it.

The idea started simple: take an old horse trailer, gut it, line it with western red cedar, drop in a wood-fired stove, and deliver it to people's properties. No permits, no permanent structure, no hassle. Just a proper sauna that shows up at your door.

We looked around and found that nobody was doing this locally. Operators from Kelowna and the Kootenays were charging delivery fees to get up here. Nobody was based on the lake, nobody knew the roads, and nobody was serving Scotch Creek or Anglemont at all.

So we built one. And now we're here.

The Trailer

Cream and brown outside.
Cedar inside.

We started with an old two-horse trailer and stripped it back to the frame. Outside, she wears a cream-and-brown vintage livery, the kind of hand-lettered look you'd find on a county-fair wagon, clean enough to sit in a lakefront yard without looking like a carnival ride.

Inside, every surface is western red cedar. Two tiered benches. A wood-fired stove. A chimney that vents through the roof. It comes up to temperature in under an hour and smells exactly like a sauna should.
