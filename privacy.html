Privacy

Your information, in plain English.

What we collect, why, and who sees it.

Who we are

Sauna Wagon is a small mobile sauna rental business based in Sorrento, BC. For any question about this page or your information, call or text (403) 809-1572.

What we collect

When you book or ask a question we collect what you give us: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, preferred dates, number of guests, and any notes you write. If you reach us through a Facebook or Instagram form or message, we receive what you submit there.

Why we collect it

Only to answer you, plan the delivery, take a deposit, and run your rental safely. We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing you did not ask for.

Who handles it

The forms on this site are delivered to us by Web3Forms, a form service. Facebook and Instagram are run by Meta. Fonts on this site load from Google Fonts.

Measuring visits and ads

This site uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta, the company behind Facebook and Instagram. It tells Meta that a device visited a page, so we can see how our ads perform and show our ads to people who have visited. It can set cookies, and Meta may receive information about your visit, such as the pages you view and your device and browser details. Meta's own privacy policy covers what Meta does with it. We only see totals, not who you are.

You can switch it off on this device. Your browser's Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track setting also switches it off.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for your booking and our business records.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call or text (403) 809-1572.
