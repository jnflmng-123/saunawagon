The Experience

Rentals & Events

We took an old horse trailer, lined it floor-to-ceiling in western red cedar, dropped in a wood-fired stove, and painted her up like she belongs at the county fair. Then we deliver it right to your property. It heats up in under an hour, smells like a proper sauna, and fits up to six people.

We fire it up, walk you through it, and leave you to it. When your session ends, we come back and haul it away. You don't touch a thing.
